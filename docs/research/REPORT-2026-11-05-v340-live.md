## Hits still do not land, and the method is byte-identical

No bot lost health and no kill line appeared. Two samples 15 s apart show
every bot at `hp=50`, including the six in `Attack`, with burst counters still
cycling. That matches the b18 result.

V3.4.0 did not change the refusal. `EntityAlive.DamageEntity` is 591 bytes
(`Code size 591 (0x24f)`) in both assemblies, and the extracted method bodies
are byte-identical (sha256
`e386b3fdc90e76a11f3263a1a466ed46b30f5232311579f9e8cf39ec19ca28f3` for each
of the b18 and V3.4.0 dumps). The early-out is at `IL_00aa`:

```
IL_00aa:  ldarg.0
IL_00ab:  ldloc.1
IL_00ac:  callvirt   instance bool EntityAlive::FriendlyFireCheck(class EntityAlive)
IL_00b1:  brtrue.s   IL_00b5
IL_00b3:  ldc.i4.m1
IL_00b4:  ret
```

So the b18 diagnosis carries over unchanged: bot fire on zombie-flag bodies
is refused by `DamageEntity` returning -1 before health is touched. This is
not a V3.4.0 regression, and it is not fixed by the drone or carry-capacity
changes.

## No horizontal movement

Across the two samples, every bot kept its X and Z exactly. The three wander
bots at separate DM points dropped from y=61.64 to y=60.33, consistent with
falling onto a surface in a chunk that is not streamed; the six in `Attack`
stayed at y=60.00. The run had no player, so chunks away from the world spawn
are unloaded, the same condition as the b18 run. Horizontal movement is
therefore still unproven in both unloaded and loaded chunks; V3.4.0 shows no
change in what this harness can observe.

## Unverified remainder

- Hits: the refusal is on the vanilla `FriendlyFireCheck` path. A per-target
  opt-in or a non-zombie body class would exercise real damage application;
  neither was built for this run.
- Movement with a real player and streamed chunks.
- Client-side rendering of the bot bodies and weapons (no client attached).
- The negative EntityClass ids and trader-body render failure from V3.1.0 b14.
